Privacy Policy

Welcome to ThreatShield AI. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy. We designed our app to minimize data collection and prioritize your security.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe in privacy by design. We do not collect unnecessary personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address to use the core features of the app. We do not read your SMS messages in the background, nor do we employ hidden tracking mechanisms.

2. Information We Do Collect

To provide and improve our services, we utilize the following:

  • Anonymous Analytics: We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with our app (e.g., feature usage frequency) to improve user experience. This data is aggregated and anonymous.
  • Crash Reporting: We use Firebase Crashlytics to monitor app stability and identify bugs. If the app crashes, diagnostic data is sent to help us fix the issue.
  • Clipboard Access: The app only accesses your clipboard when you explicitly paste text into the app for analysis. We do not monitor your clipboard in the background.

3. Message Analysis and AI Processing

ThreatShield AI allows you to analyze text, links, or messages for potential scams.

  • Message analysis is performed only after a direct user action (e.g., you pasting text and clicking analyze).
  • We utilize the Groq AI API to process and evaluate this content. When you submit text for analysis, it is securely transmitted to Groq AI servers to generate a risk assessment.
  • We do not store the contents of your analyzed messages on our servers after the analysis is complete.

4. Permissions Requested

Our app requires certain permissions to function correctly:

  • Internet Permission: Required to communicate with Groq AI API for text analysis and to send anonymous analytics and crash reports.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect the information transmitted through our app. Data sent to our AI providers and analytics services is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

6. No Selling of User Data

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information or analysis data to third parties under any circumstances.

7. Advertisements

Currently, ThreatShield AI does not display advertisements. However, future versions of the app may introduce ads. If this occurs, we will update this Privacy Policy to reflect any changes in data collection related to advertising networks.

8. User Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable data protection laws (such as GDPR or CCPA). Since we primarily collect anonymous data, we may not be able to identify your specific records. If you have concerns, you may contact us or opt-out of analytics via your device settings if supported.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@threatshield-ai.example.com.
