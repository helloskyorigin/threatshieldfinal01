Security Disclaimer

ThreatShield AI is designed to assist you in identifying potential scams and improving your cybersecurity awareness. However, it is essential to understand the limitations of our application and adhere to basic security principles.

1. AI Provides Probability, Not Certainty

The artificial intelligence utilized by ThreatShield AI analyzes patterns and language to estimate the likelihood of a message or link being a scam. It gives a probability, not an absolute certainty. Scammers constantly evolve their tactics, and the AI may not recognize new or highly sophisticated attacks. Conversely, it may occasionally flag legitimate communications.

2. Educational Purpose

ThreatShield AI is provided for educational and informational purposes only. It should be used as a supplementary tool to enhance your own vigilance, not as a definitive security barrier. You should always apply critical thinking and common sense when navigating the digital landscape.

3. Fundamental Security Rules

Regardless of what any application or AI analysis indicates, you must always adhere to these fundamental security rules:

  • Always verify financial requests: If someone asks for money, gift cards, or cryptocurrency, independently verify their identity through a known, trusted channel (e.g., calling them directly at a known number).
  • Never share OTPs (One-Time Passwords): Authentic organizations will never call, text, or email you to ask for your authorization code or OTP.
  • Never share passwords: Keep your passwords secret and use unique, strong passwords for every account.
  • Never share banking PINs: Your bank will never ask for your PIN.
  • Never trust unknown links: Do not click on links in unsolicited emails or text messages. If you receive an alert from a company, navigate to their website manually rather than using the provided link.

4. Emergency Situations

If you believe you have already fallen victim to a scam, your identity has been stolen, or you are facing an immediate threat, ThreatShield AI cannot resolve the situation. Emergency situations require the intervention of official authorities.

You should immediately:

  • Contact your bank or financial institution to freeze your accounts.
  • Report the incident to local law enforcement.
  • File a report with your national cybercrime reporting center or consumer protection agency.
